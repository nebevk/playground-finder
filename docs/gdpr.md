1. The "Big Three" GDPR Rules for Your AppA. Lawful Basis: Explicit ConsentYou cannot just collect data; you must have a "legal reason." For your app, that reason is Consent.Location: You must ask for permission to use GPS. Luckily, browsers and phones do this for you automatically.Registration: Add a checkbox to your "Sign Up" form: "I agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use." (Do not pre-check it).B. Data MinimizationOnly collect what you actually need.Do you need their full name? No. A username is enough.Do you need their birthdate? No. Just a confirmation that they are over 15 (the age of digital consent in Slovenia).C. The "Right to be Forgotten"Users must be able to leave.Feature: Add a "Delete My Account" button in their Settings.Tech Tip: With Supabase, you can set up a "Trigger" that automatically deletes all their comments and photos from the storage bucket when they delete their user ID. This keeps you 100% compliant without manual work.2. The "Photo Problem" (Critical for Playgrounds)This is your highest risk area. Photos of public spaces are generally okay in Slovenia, but photos of identifiable children without parental consent are a major GDPR violation.Your Compliance Strategy:Submission Warning: On the "Upload Photo" screen, add a clear note: "Prosimo, ne objavljajte fotografij, na katerih so prepoznavni obrazi otrok." (Please do not post photos with identifiable faces of children).Reporting System: You already planned a "Report" button. Ensure "Privacy Violation" is one of the options.Terms of Service: State clearly that users are responsible for the content they upload and that they must not upload photos of people without consent.3. Implementation Steps (Next.js & Supabase)Step 1: The Privacy Policy PageDon't hire a lawyer. Use a Privacy Policy Generator (like TermsFeed or iubenda) that has a free tier for hobby projects.What to list: Mention you use Supabase (for data), Vercel (for hosting), and Cloudflare (for spam protection).Slovenian Contact: You must provide an email address where people can contact you for data requests (e.g., [privacy@parkec.si](mailto:privacy@parkec.si)).Step 2: Cookie ConsentSince you aren't using tracking ads (like Google AdSense), you only need a very simple banner.Tool: Use the react-cookie-consent library. It’s a one-line install for Next.js.Logic: "We use essential cookies to keep you logged in. By using the app, you agree."Step 3: Secure the Database (RLS)GDPR requires "Technical Security." Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) is your best friend here. It ensures that User A cannot accidentally see or delete User B's private settings.4. Specific Slovenian Nuance: ZVOP-2Slovenia's national law (ZVOP-2) supplements GDPR.Age of Consent: In Slovenia, a minor can consent to "Information Society Services" (your app) at 15 years old.Language: Since your primary audience is Slovenian, your Privacy Policy must be available in Slovenian.5.
